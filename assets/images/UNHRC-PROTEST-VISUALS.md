The rights panels are **short original paraphrases**, with matching article references (1: dignity/equality; 3: life/liberty/security; 19: expression; 20: peaceful assembly). Primary source: [United Nations — Universal Declaration of Human Rights](https://www.un.org/en/about-us/universal-declaration-of-human-rights).

Image treatment: The original user-supplied hero poster is a *composition reference*, not a factual photographic source. No fake newspaper mastheads or attributed quotations are published. The site retains fully selectable DOM typography and functional buttons. External Pexels imagery can be swapped for licensed self-hosted optimized photos later without layout changes.
